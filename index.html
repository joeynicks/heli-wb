      About these numbers

      Verify before flight. This tool is a planning aid. Check every result against the aircraft's POH and current weight & balance record. The pilot in command is responsible for safe loading.

      Limits and arms are from the Robinson R22, R44, R44 Cadet, R44 II and R66 Pilot's Operating Handbooks (Sections 2 and 6, rev. 2014–2024). Empty weight and CG come from each aircraft's own weight & balance record.
